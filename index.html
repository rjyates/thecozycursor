About

The Cozy Cursor is a small inspiration project for people who love warm, lived-in spaces for reading, playing and working. We share original moodboards, wallpapers and setup ideas, and sometimes real products that help recreate a look.

Some of our inspiration images are created with digital tools, including AI, and are labeled as inspiration. When we recommend products, they're real items you can buy, and we say clearly when a link may earn us a commission. See our disclosure.
