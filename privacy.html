Privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026

The Cozy Cursor ("we", "us") shares cozy desk and reading nook inspiration on Pinterest and on this website. We collect as little information as possible. This page explains what that means.

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, forms, comments, analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't collect personal information through it.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and operation. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Pinterest

We use Pinterest and its official API to manage our own Pinterest account: publishing our Pins, organizing our boards and reading our own account analytics. We don't use the API to collect, store or sell information about other Pinterest users.

If you interact with us on Pinterest, Pinterest's own privacy policy applies.

Affiliate links

Some links to products are affiliate links (see our disclosure). If you click one, the store or affiliate network may use cookies or similar technology to record that the visit came from us, under their own privacy policies. We don't receive your personal details from them, only aggregated information such as the number of clicks or sales.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
