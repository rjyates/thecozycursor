how printables work

Kindle inserts are sized by model from each device's published measurements. Listings say exactly which models they fit.

  1. Buy and download the PDF right away. Nothing ships.
  2. Print at 100% (actual size) on matte photo paper or light cardstock.
  3. Cut along the solid line. Trim to the dashed line if your case is snug.
  4. Slide it behind your Kindle in its clear case, and swap it whenever the mood changes.
